Working and Studying: Understanding the Impact of Employer NOC Requirements

About this campaign

This independent campaign is collecting responses from working people and students to document the practical impact of employer NOC requirements on access to higher education. The information may be shared with an established legal organisation for the purpose of evaluating a possible legal challenge.

We believe working people deserve a genuine opportunity to pursue higher education, and that this opportunity should not depend on an employer's permission. Your voice can help build the evidence needed to make that case.

This is an independent public-interest campaign and is not an official BCI or government initiative.

Privacy: Individual responses and contact details will not be publicly published by the campaign. If you separately consent to being contacted by a legal organisation, your contact information may be shared with that organisation for that limited purpose.
